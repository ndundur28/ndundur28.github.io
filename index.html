About me

From writing the code to owning the customer.

I started my career as a Software Engineer, building features and working on applications for large enterprise clients. I enjoyed the engineering itself, but what stayed with me most were the conversations around the work. Understanding what a client was trying to achieve, how their teams worked, where their processes fell short and what they actually needed the software to do. I found myself wanting to be part of those conversations, not just the code behind the solution.

That curiosity led me to Customer Success. At an early-stage retail technology startup, I joined as a Founding Customer Success Manager and helped build the function from the ground up. I worked across the entire customer journey, from onboarding, technical implementation and training to adoption, retention, renewals and account growth. It meant getting close to how customers operated, working through implementation challenges, helping teams make technology part of their everyday workflows and building the processes needed to support them as the business grew.

At Diligent, I took that experience into the world of enterprise SaaS, managing more than 150 mid-market and enterprise accounts across multiple regions, with $2.5M in ARR under my ownership. Working with a complex GRC platform gave me a deeper understanding of the systems, processes and people behind enterprise technology. I worked closely with customers and technical teams on implementations, integrations and cloud migrations, managed escalations and owned the commercial relationship, including retention, renewals and growth.

Today, I work in AI solutions consulting, engaging with customers during pre-sales to understand their workflows and business challenges, shape solutions around their needs, build customised AI prototypes and take those solutions through implementation and deployment. Alongside this, I work with research and consulting clients seeking industry perspectives, drawing on my experience in enterprise software, customer success and the way technology businesses operate.

Looking back, my career has taken me from building software to building customer relationships, from understanding how technology works to understanding what it takes to make it work for a business. I've spent my time moving between those two perspectives, and that intersection is where I find some of my most interesting work.

01 Retention

renewal rate

Moving a portfolio off auto-renewal

Situation
The portfolio was renewing passively on auto-renewal, which left at-risk accounts to slip away.
What I did
Took individual ownership of each account, tracked health signals ahead of every renewal and stepped in early on accounts at risk.
Outcome
Renewals rose and cancellations among at-risk accounts fell from 45% to 15%.
